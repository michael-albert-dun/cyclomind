- You're trying to match a secret necklace that uses the given beads
— The starting arrangement is placed so that as many of its beads as possible are in the correct positions.
- Exchange beads on the necklace to try and do better. You can make multiple exchanges before guessing.
- After you make a guess, a helpful genie will rotate the necklace clockwise until as many beads as possible match.
- When all the beads match, you're done!
