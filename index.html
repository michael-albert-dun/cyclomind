How to play

  • Find the hidden necklace of six coloured beads.
  • Select a bead, then choose a colour from the tray.
  • Left / Right arrow keys move the selection around the ring; number keys 1-4 assign a colour.
  • Delete clears the selected bead; Enter submits a full guess.
  • A guess is checked against every rotation of itself, keeping whichever rotation best matches the secret necklace: black discs for exact matches, white discs for right colour in the wrong position. Past guesses are shown in that winning rotation.
  • All six exact matches solves it — the board locks in place with your winning guess.
